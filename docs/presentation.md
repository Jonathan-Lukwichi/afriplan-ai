## Features

### Built for how a take-off actually works
No more counting sheet by sheet. AfriPlan handles the extraction and the compliance check, so your team can focus on pricing.

**Two independent engines**
A deterministic DXF/DWG reader and an AI vision PDF reader work every job separately, so one can act as a check on the other.

**SANS 10142-1 checked automatically**
Every bill is validated against South Africa's wiring standard as part of generating it — not a step someone has to remember.

**Confidence-labelled line items**
Every quantity is tagged extracted, inferred, assumed, provisional, estimated, or manual — so you always know what to verify before it ships.

**Live supplier pricing**
Request quotes from a supplier panel for any line item, compare unit price and lead time, and apply the best one straight into the bill.

## How it works

### Every step, from a blank screen to a sent quotation
The same six screens the app itself walks you through — nothing skipped, nothing automatic that you didn't ask for.

**01 — Welcome**
The orientation screen — one button, "Upload a drawing." Nothing runs until you do.

**02 — Upload**
Choose a DXF/DWG file, a PDF drawing set, or both. A .dwg is converted to .dxf automatically behind the scenes.

**03 — Take-off**
The chosen engine reads every symbol, counts it, and matches it against a legend — seconds for DXF, up to a couple of minutes for PDF.

**04 — Compare** *(only if you ran "Both")*
Lines the DXF and PDF results up side by side, so you can see exactly where the two engines agree — and where they don't.

**05 — BoQ & quotation**
Set your markup, contingency and VAT, review the SANS-checked line items and the gap report, then download or email the quotation.

**06 — Pricing** *(optional)*
Request quotes from a supplier panel for any line item, compare them, and apply the best price straight into the bill.

## Interpreting results

### Every number tells you how sure it is
Every line item on the Take-off and BoQ pages carries one of six labels — the fastest way to know what to trust before a bill goes anywhere near a client.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Extracted** | Read directly off the drawing — the highest level of trust. |
| 🔵 **Inferred** | Worked out automatically and reliably from other extracted data. |
| 🟠 **Assumed** | Estimated using a documented assumption, written out for you to check. |
| ⚪ **Provisional** | A placeholder allowance, not measured — needs a real number before tender. |
| 🔴 **Estimated** | A rough guess with no supporting data — always worth reviewing. |
| 🔵 **Manual** | Typed in by hand, by you or a colleague. |

*Rule of thumb: green and teal, trust it. Amber, check the assumption. Grey and red, review before it goes to a client. Blue, it's your own input.*

**Never silent** — Every assumption behind an amber or red item is written out in plain language on the BoQ page's Gap report: what was assumed, why, and what to do about it — before the bill goes anywhere near a client.
