How it works

Every step, from a blank screen to a sent quotation

The same six screens the app itself walks you through — nothing skipped, nothing automatic that you didn't ask for.

01

Welcome

The orientation screen — one button, "Upload a drawing." Nothing runs until you do.

02

Upload

Choose a DXF/DWG file, a PDF drawing set, or both. A .dwg is converted to .dxf automatically behind the scenes.

03

Take-off

The chosen engine reads every symbol, counts it, and matches it against a legend — seconds for DXF, up to a couple of minutes for PDF.

04

CompareOnly if you ran "Both"

Lines the DXF and PDF results up side by side, so you can see exactly where the two engines agree — and where they don't.

05

Set your markup, contingency and VAT, review the SANS-checked line items and the gap report, then download or email the quotation.

06

Interpreting results

Every number tells you how sure it is

Every line item on the Take-off and BoQ pages carries one of six labels — the fastest way to know what to trust before a bill goes anywhere near a client.

Extracted

Read directly off the drawing — the highest level of trust.

Inferred

Worked out automatically and reliably from other extracted data.

Assumed

Estimated using a documented assumption, written out for you to check.

Provisional

A placeholder allowance, not measured — needs a real number before tender.

Estimated

A rough guess with no supporting data — always worth reviewing.

Manual

Typed in by hand, by you or a colleague.

Rule of thumb: green and teal, trust it. Amber, check the assumption. Grey and red, review before it goes to a client. Blue, it's your own input.

Never silent

Every assumption behind an amber or red item is written out in plain language on the BoQ page's Gap report — what was assumed, why, and what to do about it — before the bill goes anywhere near a client.
