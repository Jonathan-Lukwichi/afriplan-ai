# Evidence register — the claims in the AfriPlan user-guide deck

Every number or claim on the problem slides of `docs/presentation/AfriPlan_User_Guide.pptx`,
the source it was verified against on **5 October 2026**, and how. Peer-reviewed papers were
retrieved through Scite (metadata, abstract, editorial notices — none of the papers below has a
retraction or correction notice); official figures were read at the publisher's own page with
Firecrawl. Anything that could not be verified at a primary or peer-reviewed source was **removed
from the deck**, not softened.

AfriPlan's own accuracy figures (Reproduction Score etc.) are first-party measurements with the
frozen scorer (`docs/accuracy-metrics.md`), not literature claims.
